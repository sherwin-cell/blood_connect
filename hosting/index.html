Features

Everything a blood emergency needs, in one app

From finding a matching donor to confirming with the Philippine Red Cross, BloodConnect keeps every step in your pocket.

Request blood fast

Post a request with the blood type and hospital. Matching donors nearby are alerted right away.

Donate when it counts

See open requests that match your blood type and offer to donate once you're eligible.

Verified by PRC

Every account is checked by the Philippine Red Cross before anyone can donate or request blood.

How It Works

Join the community in four steps

Download the app

Install BloodConnect on your Android phone.

Register

Sign up as a blood donor, a requester, or both.

Get PRC-verified

Complete ID and identity verification with the Philippine Red Cross.

Save lives

Request blood or respond to requests that match your type.
